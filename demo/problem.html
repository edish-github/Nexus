01The problem

Operational knowledge is produced
constantly, and retained structurally nowhere.

Failure 1

Failure 2

A runbook that stopped working stays in the directory. Accumulation is mistaken for learning.

Failure 3

The past is not inspectable

You cannot ask what the system knew when it decided. The record has been written to since.

02The inversion

Retrieval is not context for the decision.
Retrieval is the decision.

Prediction

α = failed neighbours + 1
β = recovered neighbours + 1
p ~ Beta(α, β)

The k = 14 nearest precursor trajectories; their outcomes are the parameters. Emit only above 5 matches and E[p] ≥ 0.60.

Selection

θi ~ Beta(si+1, fi+1)
winner = maxi θi · σi
a contextual bandit

Argmax would be a leaderboard — a new strategy is never chosen, never gathers evidence, and expires unused. Sampling gives it turns.

04Advanced mechanisms

Six mechanisms. Each one exists because the naive version passed.

01 · MVCC

Provenance at the decision's own timestamp

Preventsreplaying at the row's latest version, which reads post-decision writes back as evidence — and makes the two panes agree.

02 · SERIALISABLE

Exactly-once over at-least-once

Preventsdouble execution on changefeed retry. The claim is FOR UPDATE, so correctness is the isolation level's, not the code's.

03 · VOCABULARY

Idempotence by construction

Preventsa rollback that races a retry leaving the fleet carrying two remediations instead of none. Steps declare a target, never a delta.

04 · LINEAGE

Merge refuses relatives, and inherits conservatively

Preventsfamily collapse — a mutation sits at its parent's position, so nearest-neighbour returns the parent every time.

05 · SHADOW

Fractional trials for counterfactuals

Preventscrediting a strategy that was never executed. Weight 0.30, and a human's rejection is scored rather than discarded.

06 · RETENTION

The GC window is a correctness parameter

Preventsa silently shortened window breaking replay — a TRUNCATE once cut 7 days to 75 minutes and every test kept passing.
